Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Something else on your mind? Call 905-277-2800.

Do you cater small parties?

Yes. Our drop-off platters work for any size, and our luxury home menu starts at 10 guests.

Do you provide servers and setup?

For full-service events our team sets up, serves buffet or plated courses and clears away afterwards. Drop-off orders arrive ready to serve.

Can you handle dietary needs?

Tell us about allergies, vegetarian guests or other needs in the quote form and we’ll plan the menu around them.

Which areas do you serve?

Mississauga, Etobicoke and Toronto, and often Oakville, Brampton and the rest of the GTA. Just ask.

How do I get a price?

Send the quote form or call 905-277-2800. We’ll suggest a menu and give you a clear price, with no obligation.
